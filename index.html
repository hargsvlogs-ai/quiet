What makes Quiet different

Six things nobody else gets right.

01

Pay once. Forever.

One price, no subscription, no feature clawback after three days. Buy it, own it. The way software used to work.

02

Glanceable essentials

Pull down for weather, next meeting, important messages, reminders. Focus without forgetting the rest of your life.

Optional 24-hour cooldown before you can disable Quiet or change launchers. The whole point is that future-you can't undo present-you.

12 or 24-hour clock, font size slider, high-contrast mode, dyslexia-friendly font option. From day one, not version four.
